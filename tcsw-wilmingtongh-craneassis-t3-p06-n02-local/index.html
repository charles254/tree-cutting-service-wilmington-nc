On-Site Commercial Chipping & Landscape Mulch Benefits

High-capacity wood chippers process massive branch loads into clean, coarse wood chips in minutes. Homeowners can retain fresh wood chips on-site for weed suppression, soil moisture retention, and garden pathway groundcover.

Hardwood Firewood Seasoning & Timber Repurposing

Mature oak, hickory, and cherry trees produce dense, clean-burning firewood. Logs can be bucked into standard 16-to-18-inch stove lengths on-site or hauled to local sawmills and firewood processors for seasoned winter fuel.

  • Bucking hardwood trunks into fireplace-ready lengths for homeowner seasoning
  • Transporting premium sawlogs to local Coastal Carolina specialty woodturners and mills
  • Diverting 100 percent of clean tree biomass away from municipal landfills
  • Supporting local carbon sequestration and sustainable urban forest recycling
